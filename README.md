# Customer Churn Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer churn data using SQL and Microsoft Power BI to understand customer retention, churn patterns, service usage, and factors associated with customer churn.

The analysis combines SQL-based data analysis with an interactive Power BI dashboard to convert customer data into meaningful business insights and actionable retention recommendations.

## Objectives

- Measure overall customer churn and retention performance.
- Identify churn patterns across different customer attributes.
- Analyze customer service usage and its relationship with churn.
- Compare churned customers across states and plan types.
- Analyze account length patterns among churned customers.
- Translate analytical findings into actionable customer retention strategies.

## Tools & Technologies

- SQL
- Microsoft Power BI
- DAX
- Data Analysis
- Data Visualization
- Interactive Dashboard

## Key KPIs

- Total Customers: 3,333
- Active Customers: 2,850
- Churned Customers: 483
- Churn Rate: 14%
- Average Day Minutes: 179.78
- Average Service Calls: 1.56

## Analysis Performed

### 1. Customer Churn Overview

Analyzed the overall customer base to measure active customers, churned customers, and the overall churn rate.

### 2. Churn by State

Analyzed churned customers across states and identified the top states with higher numbers of churned customers.

### 3. International Plan Analysis

Compared churned customers based on international plan subscription status to understand its relationship with customer churn.

### 4. Voice Mail Plan Analysis

Compared churned customers based on voice mail plan subscription status.

### 5. Account Length Analysis

Created account-length bins and analyzed the distribution of churned customers across different account-tenure ranges.

### 6. Call Usage Analysis

Compared total day, evening, and international call usage between churned and non-churned customers.

### 7. Customer Service Calls

Analyzed churned customers by the number of customer service calls to identify potential service-related churn patterns.

## Key Insights

- 483 customers were identified as churned out of 3,333 total customers.
- The overall churn rate is approximately 14%.
- Churn varies across states, with certain states showing comparatively higher churn counts.
- Customer plan characteristics such as international and voice mail plans were analyzed to identify differences in churn behavior.
- Account-length analysis shows that churn is concentrated within specific tenure ranges.
- Customer service call activity provides an additional indicator for understanding potential churn behavior.
- Usage patterns across day, evening, and international calls were compared between churned and non-churned customers.

## Business Recommendations

- Prioritize retention efforts in states with higher churn counts.
- Monitor customers with repeated customer service interactions and investigate unresolved service issues.
- Identify high-risk customer segments using plan type, tenure, and usage behavior.
- Develop targeted retention offers for customers showing early signs of churn.
- Continuously monitor churn KPIs through the Power BI dashboard to support proactive retention decisions.

## Dashboard

The Power BI dashboard provides interactive analysis of customer churn using KPIs, filters, and visualizations covering customer demographics, plans, account length, call usage, and service interactions.

## Project Outcome

The analysis transformed customer data into an interactive churn analytics dashboard, enabling identification of churn patterns and supporting data-driven customer retention and service improvement decisions.
